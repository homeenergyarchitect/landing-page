Why your current solar setup isn't paying off

Default Inverter Profiles

Installers are incentivized to get in and out fast. Most batteries run basic self-consumption modes rather than dynamically charging or discharging around variable Wholesale or Peak rates.

📉

Outdated Retail Tariffs

Feed-in tariffs have dropped sharply across AEMO states. Staying on legacy single-rate or poorly matched Time-of-Use plans actively drains your solar ROI.

Conflicting Incentives

Energy retailers want to sell you grid power, and solar installers want to sell you hardware. Nobody is looking at your ongoing operational efficiency.
