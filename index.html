Where energy setups lose their edge

Hardware gets installed for day one, but retail power plans and household habits shift every month.

Factory Default Settings

Installers focus on mounting hardware safely—not ongoing tariff matching. Without custom schedule tuning, batteries often charge from expensive grid power or empty too early in the evening.

Complex Data & Spreadsheets

Finding the right electricity plan requires matching 5-minute smart meter data against dozens of peak/off-peak rates and mid-day solar tariffs. It is tedious work that most homeowners do not have time for.

Changing Energy Tariffs

As solar export rates drop and dynamic pricing expands, settings that saved money two years ago may now cause quiet losses. Without periodic reviews, savings slowly drift away.
